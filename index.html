Before finals

Open points

"Backed by DGLD" needs a definition

How much DGLD sits behind each card, and whether that changes with rarity. This is crypto-asset marketing under MiCA, so legal should check the wording before anything goes out.

PAMP marks are placeholders

The model garbles logos. For the finals, the PAMP and Lady Fortuna cards get re-rendered with the official artwork as a reference image. One draft stamped a competitor's name and was redone.

Redos already queued

The Strip came out as a ring in most styles, so it needs a clearer prompt. The DGLD medallion follows the reference but the D is slightly soft. For finals it gets a tighter edit pass.

IP was cleared out

The trophy is now a generic loving cup, the medal has no rings, and the jewelled egg carries no Fabergé name. The Ethereum and Bitcoin marks are open, but it's worth a final check with SETZ.

Facts to verify

Every caption is written to be checkable. Before anything ships, check the Tut mask weight (10.23 kg), the 1912 medal fact and the art-history dates against a source.

Vreneli and artworks

The 2025 Vreneli design belongs to Swissmint (artist Chiara Principe), so SETZ needs Swissmint's permission to sell it as an NFT. The Klimt, Cellini and Ghiberti works are public domain, but museum photos can carry their own rights. We render our own version, so this is low risk, but it's worth a check.
